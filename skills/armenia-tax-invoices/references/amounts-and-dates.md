# Amounts and service dates

Use this reference when an amount must be derived or a service date needs resolving. Already-reviewed period inputs can be used directly. Preserve the established invoice pattern and optional blanks; calculations and supporting evidence belong in a private worksheet, not added invoice prose.

## Establish the period and amount

Match the target to the contract/service evidence and the user's reviewed instructions. A bank receipt's memo can identify a service month, but its payment date does not establish service completion. An issue date in the following month also does not prove which service period an invoice covers.

Use the verified period amount. Fees, partial payments, changing monthly fees, currency basis, and contract phases may prevent copying the prior month's number. Retain original currency, amount, quote unit, rate, rate date, recognition/service date, and evidence privately when converting.

Use decimal arithmetic: `AMD = foreign amount × AMD rate ÷ currency quote unit`. Verify the historical CBA quotation and its unit from the [CBA](https://www.cba.am/) or the [rate.am CBA archive](https://www.rate.am/hy/armenian-dram-exchange-rates/central-bank-armenia). A commercial bank's conversion quote is not automatically the CBA basis. Preserve the verified invoice precision; do not reuse whole-dram tax-return rounding without establishing that it applies to the invoice.

If the requested number is already reviewed and supported, do not expand the task into recomputing old invoices. If a material difference affects the new amount, identify the missing basis before entering that amount; do not silently reuse either disputed number. Complete independent template and duplicate checks while resolving it.

The optional `armenia-tax-income` receipt-date helper does not determine service dates. Do not relabel its inputs to simulate another recognition method. Use a separate reviewed calculation when the method differs. Issuing an invoice does not authorize changing a tax return.

## Period-specific law checks

Source review date: 2026-10-11. Recheck the effective text for the transaction's period when applying these provisions.

- [Tax Code Articles 40 and 56](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/230455/latest) address service timing and issuance. Determine actual completion or the applicable contract phase; subscription and other specific cases have their own rules. Do not assume a universal grace period until the next month's 20th for invoices.
- [Law HO-234-N, Article 2 and commencement provisions](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/226008) amended Article 16 from 2026-07-01. For service transactions under the amended rule, establish the date under Article 40 and the CBA previous-working-day basis, subject to the article's explicit exceptions. An invoice's issue date or a payment date is not automatically the required service date.
- [The earlier Tax Code text](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/226064) used a different Article 16 basis for specified resident/nonresident transactions. Do not apply July's method to an earlier transaction without checking the applicable text and case.
- [Tax Code Articles 55 and 67](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/230455/latest) distinguish account certificates and tax invoices. Preserve the verified special-regime document type and treatment; a foreign buyer alone does not establish another classification.

Keep statutory rules, verified contract facts, and user-reported account conventions distinct. If a concrete new invoice requires resolving a conflict, explain that specific field and its evidence gap. A difference in an old invoice is not authorization to correct, cancel, or reissue it.
