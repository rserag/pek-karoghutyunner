# Form 244: annual social and applicable stamp-fee calculation

Research checked 2026-10-05. Portal code 244 is historical catalogue evidence; verify the current number, Armenian title and reporting-year version before entry.

## Version gate

The [N 749-N official form rules](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/226558), annex 3, contain social lines 6–10 and a revised stamp section, lines 11–12. [Order N 1437-N as amended](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/219232) takes effect July 1, 2026 and applies to reporting periods after that date. Its original January 1 date was amended; do not use the original date from a stale copy.

**The first annual year using the revised form remains unresolved in this skill.** A calendar 2026 reporting year spans July 1, whereas calendar 2027 begins after it. Neither the effective date alone nor filing in 2027 proves which annual version applies to 2026. Before preparing either year's portal fields, inspect the year-specific catalogue/help and verify the annual interpretation in official SRC guidance. Record the decisive evidence. Do not infer that 2025 uses the newest stamp section or that 2026 necessarily does. If unresolved, complete the independently supported worksheet and keep the uncertain fields/version unfinalized.

## Social applicability and calculation

[Funded-pension law articles 5–7](https://old.arlis.am/documentview.aspx?docID=224823) establish the social payer and payment rules. Mandatory participation generally covers entrepreneurs born January 1, 1974 or later; earlier cohorts pay if they opted in. Residents who are foreign citizens/stateless persons generally follow the same rules, subject to treaties. Reaching pension age alone does not end participation: verify the applicable stopping application and effective month.

For turnover-tax activity, annex 3 maps social liability to **line 8**, at AMD 5,000 for each applicable month. Its instructions say turnover entrepreneurs fill only this line in the social section; inspect the portal's calculated totals rather than manually forcing lines 6, 7 or 9. General-system line 6 is a profit-tax base, not turnover receipts. Keep social liability separate from fixed profit tax even when their amounts coincide.

Being outside the social-payment system does not by itself remove a stamp-fee reporting obligation. If the verified revised version requires form 244 for stamp reporting, prepare that section even when social liability does not apply. Verify from the selected form's official help whether its social fields should be blank or zero; do not infer field treatment from exemption alone. If that help is unavailable, preserve the supported stamp worksheet and leave the social-field treatment unresolved.

Build an independent social-month worksheet with participation status, applicable regime, registration/status changes, stopping application where relevant, and authoritative grounds for each inclusion/exclusion. **A social suspension-month rule equivalent to profit-tax Article 125(3) was not established by this research.** Before excluding suspended months, verify the applicable social rule through current official SRC/form guidance; do not copy the profit worksheet. Zero income, combined salary/business caps and refunds also require their actual legal treatment, not an automatic twelve-month amount. Unresolved cases remain unfinalized.

Annual personalized reporting is due April 20 of the following year under [personalized-accounting law 11(1)](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/172572/print/act); funded-pension law 7(4) ties entrepreneur social payment to the profit-tax payment deadline. Recheck the actual year and any applicable extension.

## Stamp base and fee

If the selected version requires a stamp section, **line 11** is the annual sales-turnover sum reflected in the relevant quarterly turnover returns; **line 12** is the resulting statutory stamp fee. Use each quarter's latest accepted original/correction once, with report references and a whole-dram annual rollup. Unsaved drafts, quarterly tax amounts, bank receipts alone and line 107 are not the stamp base.

The [current stamp-fee statute, articles 9, 11, 12 and 15](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/227002) supplies the annual base, exemptions, bands and payment rule. No applicable annual base means no stamp obligation; foreign-residence exemptions and other claimed relief require evidence. For supported turnover-only years, historical rate facts are:

| Annual base, AMD | 2025 fee, AMD | From January 1, 2026, fee, AMD |
|---|---:|---:|
| Positive base up to 2,400,000 | 18,000 | 12,000 |
| 2,400,001–6,000,000 | 24,000 | 12,000 |
| 6,000,001–12,000,000 | 48,000 | 12,000 |
| 12,000,001 or more | 120,000 | 120,000 |

Sources: [2025 consolidated article 12](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/199397), [HՕ-477-N amendment and effective date](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/218648). These are year-specific facts to verify, not default rates for a new case. The stamp payment deadline for entrepreneurs follows the annual profit-tax deadline under current article 15(2). The existence of a stamp obligation does not establish which historical form carried it.

If there are mixed regimes, derive their proper bases and annual interaction from official instructions instead of applying a turnover-only rollup. This first version does not finalize that treatment.
