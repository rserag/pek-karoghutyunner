# Form 240: annual profit-tax calculation

Research checked 2026-10-05. Recheck the official consolidated act, historical version and transitions for the requested reporting year.

## Title and treatment

Form 240 is the calculation for a resident profit-tax payer or a nonresident operating through a permanent establishment. Its relevant turnover-entrepreneur amount is section 1, line 107. The current Armenian label is **Շրջանառության հարկով հարկվող գործունեության տեսակներով զբաղված անհատ ձեռնարկատերերի շահութահարկի գումարը**. Older versions also mention patent tax and notaries; those words do not authorize their use for later periods.

The [official form and completion rules, N 749-N](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/226558), annex 1 paragraph 36, link line 107 to Tax Code 125(3): fixed profit tax is AMD 5,000 per applicable month for turnover-tax business activity, regardless of the number of activities. This is a liability amount, not turnover and not the unpaid balance. Do not reduce it by payments already made. Twelve applicable months give AMD 60,000 only after the year and month count have been established.

## Profit-tax month worksheet

Under [Tax Code 125(3)](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/214687/latest), a properly submitted suspension declaration excludes full calendar months within the qualifying suspended interval. Ordinary suspension begins no earlier than the declaration's submission date. The statute has a separate rule for a newly registered entrepreneur who submits a suspension declaration by the end of the day after registration; inspect it when relevant.

For each month record registration status, turnover-regime applicability, suspension/resumption effective and submission dates, whether a complete calendar month is excluded, and the reason/source. A partial active month is not prorated; lack of receipts does not itself create a suspension exclusion. Do not reuse this statutory profit-tax exemption as authority for social payments.

The first version handles exclusively turnover-tax business treatment. Verify which identification fields and calculated fields the selected form actually requires. Do not populate general-system income/profit rows merely by copying turnover returns, or assign zero to unsupported income categories without evidence. Mixed/general-system periods require their own calculations and current instructions.

## Deadlines and checks

[Tax Code 134(1) and 136(4)](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/214687/latest) set annual filing and fixed profit-tax payment by April 20 inclusive of the following year. Determine any legally applicable nonworking-day extension or special rule for the actual due date; do not conflate filing acceptance with payment. The announced [HՕ-385-N amendment](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/228632), effective February 1, 2027, changes tax-agent provisions in 134(2)/136(2), not these annual turnover-entrepreneur provisions.

Before saving, compare line 107 with the independent profit-month worksheet and verify form/year/version. Reopen the saved draft to confirm the same amount persisted. Retain the gross liability and any payments/balance reconciliation as separate evidence.
