# Form 201 eligibility and deadlines

Research checked **2026-10-05**. Refresh the official rules for the requested year and event; distinguish an enacted future change from a rule already effective. This skill covers ordinary individual entrepreneurs. The public government service identifies [form 201 and its portal workflow](https://hartak.am/services/2e560b1b-1bfd-4017-a180-72945fec90a0/online/). The binding declaration form is approved by [SRC order N191-N](https://arlis.am/hy/acts/209026).

## Select the statutory case

[Tax Code Article 254](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/223082) supplies the following cases. Use the [official legacy view](https://old.arlis.am/DocumentView.aspx?docid=223082) if the current ARLIS page is difficult to read. A deadline stated as the twentieth day following an event includes that twentieth day. Do not count the event as day one or assume a weekend/holiday changes the cutoff without verifying an applicable rule.

| Case | Effective date | Filing window or condition |
|---|---|---|
| Annual election, Article 254(1)(1) | January 1 of the requested tax year | By February 20 of that year inclusive. |
| New registration, (1)(2) | Individual-entrepreneur registration date | By the twentieth day following registration inclusive. |
| Loss of microenterprise status, (1)(3) | Date the taxpayer ceased to qualify as microenterprise | From that date through the twentieth day following it inclusive; turnover eligibility must independently hold. |
| Voluntary transition from microenterprise, (1)(4) | Date stated in the declaration | Cannot be earlier than the twentieth day before submission. If micro status ended before the chosen date, apply the loss-of-status case and submit the required new declaration; the earlier declaration is disregarded for that case. |
| Restart, (1)(5) | Documented resumption date | Applies to a taxpayer that did not operate/had suspended activity through February 20 inclusive. Submit by the twentieth day following resumption inclusive. A later restart does not itself create a fresh election window for a taxpayer that operated earlier in that year. |
| Re-registration after deregistration before the tax year, (1)(6) | Re-registration date | By the twentieth day following re-registration inclusive. |
| Re-registration after deregistration during the same tax year, (1)(7) | Re-registration date | By the twentieth day following re-registration inclusive, except when the taxpayer had VAT-payer status in that tax year before deregistration. |

These are conditional ways to obtain the status through the end of the year, subject to a later event ending it. Establish the actual registration/suspension/resumption and regime record; do not manufacture an event or effective date to fit a deadline. If the relevant deadline was missed, inspect any genuinely applicable alternative statutory case. Otherwise explain the general-regime/VAT consequence under Article 59 and seek qualified resolution of an exceptional claim rather than filing a false date. Electronic acceptance of a late document is not proof that a late election legally took effect.

## Eligibility evidence and attestations

Ordinary eligibility requires prior-year statutory sales turnover across all activities **not exceeding AMD 115 million**, including turnover attributable to microenterprise activities. Do not subtract costs or use only one activity, one currency, or net bank credits. Reconcile the annual total from filed returns and source records using the applicable recognition rules.

Verify the exclusions in Article 254(3) and the version of N191-N effective on the filing date. The form's negative attestations include these material groups:

- Financial and specified regulated activities: banks, credit/insurance entities and intermediaries, investment entities and securities professionals, pawnshops, currency trading, funds/managers, payment institutions; gambling/lottery activities under the applicable version; notaries, audit organizations and legal-service providers; cryptoasset service providers under the defined law.
- Activity classes **69** (legal/accounting), **70** (head offices/management consulting), **78.2** and **78.3** (labor provision). The declaration additions for these classes apply from **2025-07-01**, per [N818-N](https://arlis.am/hy/acts/209023). Check actual services, not a convenient code label.
- Joint-activity contracts and goods-supply commission/agency contracts with the agent acting in its own name. Do not turn this specific goods-supply restriction into a blanket ban on every service intermediary arrangement.
- Statutory related parties under Article 30(1), subject to the documented suspension/no-actual-activity exception; and parties deemed related by the tax authority under Article 30(2), whose combined relevant turnover must not exceed the threshold. These rules differ; do not collapse them into one threshold-only check.

The ordinary form is not a shortcut to the separate public-catering exception. If that activity requires the exceptional regime, verify the separate statutory case and form before proceeding.

## Status throughout the year

Article 255 ends ordinary turnover status when current-year sales turnover exceeds AMD 115 million, an applicable disqualifying event occurs, or a voluntary VAT election takes effect. Establish the event's statutory date: most apply from the event, while the tax-authority-deemed related-party case has its own retroactive rule and appeal provisions. Article 255(2) requires notification for specified involuntary exit events by the twentieth day following the event inclusive. Verify Article 59 for VAT registration and the transaction-specific consequences.

Article 253 distinguishes individual entrepreneurs: turnover tax replaces VAT, while profit-tax obligations remain under their own rules. That regime relationship is not a universal VAT exemption for imports or every other transaction. Form 201 neither determines an activity's rate nor proves that a separate tax obligation is absent.
